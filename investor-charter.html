Investor Charter

Published in compliance with the SEBI Investor Charter for Research Analysts. It sets out what we do, what you are entitled to, the timelines we are bound by, and what we expect from you in return.

Reference

This charter should be read with the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 and the circulars issued under them. Where anything here conflicts with the regulations as they stand from time to time, the regulations prevail.

Vision

To make independent, written and reviewable securities research available to Indian investors, so that every decision taken on our work can be understood and re-examined by the person taking it.

Mission

  • To produce research based on primary sources, with the reasoning and the evidence disclosed rather than asserted.
  • To state the risk and the point of invalidation on every published idea before stating any upside.
  • To disclose all conflicts of interest fully and promptly.
  • To deal with every investor fairly, and to resolve grievances within the timelines set out below.

Details of business transacted by the research analyst

Wealthymind Research Private Limited is a research analyst entity. Our business consists of:

  • preparing and publishing research reports on securities listed on recognised Indian stock exchanges;
  • providing research-based recommendations on such securities to subscribers of our plans;
  • publishing general market, sector and macroeconomic commentary.

We do not undertake portfolio management, do not provide personalised investment advice, do not execute trades, do not take custody of client funds or securities, and do not hold any power of attorney over client accounts.

Services provided to investors

  • Research reports and recommendations on securities, distributed identically to all subscribers of the relevant plan.
  • Disclosure of the basis of each recommendation, including the thesis, the evidence relied on, the risk and invalidation level, the timeframe and the review date.
  • Conflict of interest disclosures in each report, covering analyst and firm holdings in the subject security.
  • Review, re-rating and closure notices on previously published ideas.
  • Grievance handling through the process set out below.

Rights of investors

  • Right to receive fair, honest and unbiased research.
  • Right to receive complete and true disclosure of all material information, including conflicts of interest.
  • Right to receive the terms of service, fees and refund terms in writing before payment.
  • Right to privacy and confidentiality of your information.
  • Right to have grievances addressed within the stated timelines, and to escalate to SEBI or to online dispute resolution if not satisfied.
  • Right to be informed that we can make no guarantee of returns.

What we expect from you — the Do's

  • Deal only with a SEBI registered research analyst. Verify our registration number (INH000XXXXXX) on the SEBI website.
  • Pay fees only into the bank account named on an invoice issued by Wealthymind Research Private Limited, and always insist on a receipt.
  • Read the research report in full, including the risk, invalidation and disclosure sections, before acting.
  • Keep a written record of the terms of service and of all correspondence with us.
  • Tell us promptly if your contact details change, so that research and notices continue to reach you.

And the Don'ts

  • Do not deal with anyone offering assured, guaranteed or risk-free returns on securities. No one can lawfully promise this.
  • Do not share your trading password, demat credentials or OTP with us or with anyone else. We will never ask.
  • Do not pay fees in cash, or into any personal account or wallet.
  • Do not hand over funds or securities to us for trading or management — we are not permitted to accept them.
  • Do not act on a recommendation you have not read and understood, or one circulated through an unofficial group claiming to represent us.
