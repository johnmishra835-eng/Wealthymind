Questions

About subscriptions

Is there a free trial?

We do not run free trials of live research. We will, on request, share a redacted sample note so you can see the format and depth before deciding.

Can I get a refund mid-term?

Refund eligibility, the pro-rata basis and the processing timeline are all set out in our terms and refund policy. Dissatisfaction with market outcomes is not itself a ground for refund, and we say so plainly rather than implying otherwise.

Do you offer a lifetime plan?

No. Research analysts may only bill for a limited period in advance, so any "lifetime" securities research subscription should be treated with suspicion.

Will you tell me how much to invest in an idea?

No. Position sizing depends on your finances, which we do not know and are not permitted to advise on. Our notes state the invalidation level; converting that into a rupee amount is your decision, or one for a registered investment adviser.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us how you invest. If none of our plans suit you, we will say so rather than sell you the nearest one.
