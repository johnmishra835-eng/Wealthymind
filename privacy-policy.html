Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do about it. Last updated DD Month YYYY.

Have this reviewed before you publish it

This policy is drafted to reflect the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and SEBI record-keeping obligations, but it is not legal advice and the DPDP rules are still being operationalised. Have it reviewed, and complete the placeholders, before relying on it.

Who we are

Wealthymind Research Private Limited, CIN U66190GJ2025PTC170746, registered office Registered office address, City, Gujarat – PIN, is the data fiduciary for the personal data described here. For any privacy question, contact support@example.com.

What we collect

  • Identity and contact data — name, email address, mobile number, postal address.
  • Subscription and billing data — the plan you hold, invoices, payment references and GST details where applicable. We do not store full card numbers; payments are processed by a payment gateway.
  • Onboarding data — information we are required to collect before providing research, including any suitability or risk-profile declarations, and KYC records where applicable.
  • Correspondence — enquiries, support messages and grievance records.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and pages visited, where our hosting provider logs it.

We never ask for, and you should never send us, your trading password, demat credentials or any OTP.

Why we use it

  • To deliver the research you have subscribed to.
  • To invoice you and maintain accounting records.
  • To meet our obligations as a research analyst entity, including record retention and responding to a regulator.
  • To answer your enquiries and handle grievances.
  • To send service messages about your subscription. Marketing messages are sent only where you have not opted out, and every such message carries an unsubscribe route.

Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give at onboarding, the performance of our contract with you, and our legal and regulatory obligations. Where processing rests on consent, you may withdraw it — though doing so may mean we can no longer provide the service.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • service providers who operate our email delivery, hosting, payments and accounting, under contract and only as needed;
  • our auditors, lawyers and compliance consultants, where required;
  • SEBI, a stock exchange, a court or any other authority where we are legally obliged to disclose.

How long we keep it

Research analysts are required to retain records — including research reports, their rationale and client communications — for a minimum period prescribed by the applicable regulations, currently 5 years (confirm against the current regulation). We keep personal data for that period and for as long afterwards as any tax, accounting or legal requirement demands, then delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

  • Access — ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete data we no longer need, subject to our retention obligations.
  • Withdrawal of consent — where processing rests on consent.
  • Grievance — complain to us and, if unsatisfied, to the Data Protection Board once constituted.
  • Nomination — nominate someone to exercise your rights.

Write to support@example.com to exercise any of these. We respond within N working days.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access control on a need-to-know basis, encryption of the site in transit, and periodic review of who holds access. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the relevant authority as required.

Cookies

This website sets no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. We store a single preference in your browser's local storage to remember your light or dark theme choice, and our host may log standard request data. If analytics are added later, this section and a consent notice will be updated first.

Children

Our services are not offered to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We will publish any revised policy on this page with a new "last updated" date, and notify material changes to your registered email.
