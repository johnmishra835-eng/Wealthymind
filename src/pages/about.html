A research house, not a tip service

Wealthymind Research Private Limited was incorporated in Gujarat as a research analyst entity. We exist to do one thing well: produce written, defensible research on Indian listed securities.

Why we set this up

The problem with a tip

Indian retail investors are not short of recommendations. They are short of reasoning. A level circulated without a thesis cannot be evaluated, cannot be sized correctly, and cannot be reviewed when the facts change — because nobody wrote down what the facts were supposed to be.

Wealthymind Research was built around the opposite discipline. Every note states what must be true, what would prove it wrong, and when it will be revisited. That makes our work slower and our coverage smaller. We think that is the correct trade.

How we are governed

We operate under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 and the circulars issued under them. That regime requires a written internal policy on conflicts of interest, mandatory disclosure of analyst and firm holdings in covered securities, retention of every published note and its rationale, named officers accountable for compliance and for investor grievances, and a documented grievance process with defined timelines.

It also means hard limits on what we may do. We are a research analyst, not a portfolio manager and not an investment adviser: we publish research, we do not manage your money, we do not handle your funds or securities, and we do not provide personalised advice tailored to your individual circumstances.

Principles

Five rules we hold ourselves to

These are not marketing lines. They are the standing instructions our analysts work under, and the basis on which you should hold us to account.

  1. Evidence over narrative

    A claim that cannot be traced to a filing, a disclosure or an observable price series does not go into a note.

  2. The bear case is written too

    Every note contains the strongest argument against its own conclusion. If we cannot construct one, we have not understood the idea well enough to publish it.

  3. Risk is defined before upside

    The invalidation level is fixed first. A note without a stated point of being wrong is an opinion, not research.

  4. Silence is not an exit

    Open ideas are formally closed or re-rated on their review date. We do not let calls quietly disappear from the record.

  5. No performance theatre

    We publish no win rates, no return claims and no back-tested track records, because none of them predict your outcome and all of them invite you to skip the reasoning.

Questions

Common questions about the firm

Do you guarantee returns?

No. Nobody lawfully can. Securities market investments carry market risk, and any entity promising you an assured or guaranteed return should be treated as a red flag and reported.

Will you manage my portfolio or place trades for me?

No. As a research analyst entity we are not permitted to do either. We publish research; every decision and every order remains yours, placed through your own broker.

Is your research personalised to my situation?

No. Our notes are general research made available to all subscribers of a plan. They take no account of your income, liabilities, tax position, time horizon or risk tolerance. If you need advice specific to your circumstances, consult a SEBI registered investment adviser.

Do your analysts hold the stocks they write about?

Sometimes, and where they do it is disclosed inside the note itself, along with the firm's own holdings, as our regulations require. See our disclosures page for the full policy.

How do I complain if something goes wrong?

Write to our grievance address first — we are bound to acknowledge and resolve within defined timelines. If you remain dissatisfied you can escalate to SEBI via SCORES or to conciliation and arbitration via the Smart ODR portal. The full process and timelines are on our grievance redressal page.
