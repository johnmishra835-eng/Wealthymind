Grievance redressal

If we have got something wrong, this page tells you exactly who to write to, how long we have to respond, and where to go if our response does not satisfy you.

Step by step

How to raise a complaint

  1. Write to us

    Email {{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}} with your name, registered email or mobile number, the plan concerned and a description of the issue. Attach any relevant invoice or correspondence. We acknowledge within 1 working day.

    2. {{#COMPLIANCE_OFFICER}}

  2. Escalate to the Compliance Officer

    If you have had no response within 3 working days, or you are not satisfied with it, escalate to our Compliance Officer: {{COMPLIANCE_OFFICER}}.

    3. {{/COMPLIANCE_OFFICER}} {{#PRINCIPAL_OFFICER}}

  3. Escalate to the Principal Officer

    If the matter is still unresolved, write to our Principal Officer: {{PRINCIPAL_OFFICER}}.

    4. {{/PRINCIPAL_OFFICER}}

  4. Escalate to SEBI on SCORES

    You may lodge a complaint with SEBI on the SCORES portal at scores.sebi.gov.in. SCORES is free to use and complaints are tracked to closure. SEBI's toll-free helplines are 1800 22 7575 and 1800 266 7575.

  5. Online dispute resolution

    If the grievance remains unresolved, you may initiate conciliation and, if needed, arbitration through the Smart ODR portal at smartodr.in, established under the SEBI online dispute resolution framework.

Before you escalate

Please give us the chance to fix it first. Include your registered email, the invoice number if the complaint concerns a payment, and what outcome you are looking for. It materially speeds up resolution, and we would rather resolve a matter than have you chase it through a regulator.
