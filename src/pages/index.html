Our method

How a note gets published

Six steps, in order, every time. Nothing reaches a subscriber that has not been through all of them.

  1. Universe screen

    We start from a defined, liquid universe and filter on quantitative criteria before any judgement is applied.

  2. Primary reading

    Annual reports, quarterly filings and exchange disclosures are read in full — not summarised second-hand.

  3. Thesis drafting

    The analyst writes the case and, separately, the strongest case against it. Both go into the note.

  4. Risk definition

    Before a level is published we fix the invalidation point and the position sizing assumption it depends on.

  5. Internal review

    A second reader checks the arithmetic, the sourcing and the disclosure section against our own checklist.

  6. Publication and follow-through

    The note is released with its review date attached, and it is formally closed or re-rated — never left to lapse silently.

Our standards

Judge us on process, not on promises

We do not publish return claims, success rates or performance records, because no honest research firm can guarantee outcomes. What we can commit to is how the work is done.

Every call is written down

No verbal tips and no unexplained levels. If we cannot write the reasoning down and defend it, we do not publish it.

Risk comes before target

The invalidation level is set before the upside case is stated, so the downside is never an afterthought.

Conflicts are disclosed

Analyst and firm holdings in any covered security are disclosed in the note itself, as our regulations require.
