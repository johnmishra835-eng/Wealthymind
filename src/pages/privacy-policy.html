What we collect

  • Identity and contact data — name, email address, mobile number, postal address.

Why we use it

  • To deliver the research you have subscribed to.
  • To invoice you and maintain accounting records.
  • To meet our obligations as a research analyst entity, including record retention and responding to a regulator.
  • To answer your enquiries and handle grievances.
  • To send service messages about your subscription. Marketing messages are sent only where you have not opted out, and every such message carries an unsubscribe route.

Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give at onboarding, the performance of our contract with you, and our legal and regulatory obligations. Where processing rests on consent, you may withdraw it — though doing so may mean we can no longer provide the service.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • service providers who operate our email delivery, hosting, payments and accounting, under contract and only as needed;
  • our auditors, lawyers and compliance consultants, where required;
  • SEBI, a stock exchange, a court or any other authority where we are legally obliged to disclose.

How long we keep it

Research analysts are required to retain records — including research reports, their rationale and client communications — for a minimum period prescribed by the applicable regulations, currently 5 years (confirm against the current regulation). We keep personal data for that period and for as long afterwards as any tax, accounting or legal requirement demands, then delete or anonymise it.

  • Withdrawal of consent — where processing rests on consent.
  • Grievance — complain to us and, if unsatisfied, to the Data Protection Board once constituted.
  • Nomination — nominate someone to exercise your rights.
