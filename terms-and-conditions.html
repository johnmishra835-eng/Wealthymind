Refund & cancellation policy

We would rather be clear about this up front than argue about it later.

When a refund is available

  • Duplicate or excess payment. Refunded in full.
  • Payment made but service never activated. Refunded in full.
  • We terminate the plan for our own reasons. Refunded pro-rata for the unused part of the billing period.
  • Cancellation within N days of a new subscription, before any research has been delivered. Refunded in full, less any payment gateway charges actually incurred.

When a refund is not available

  • Where research for the period has already been delivered and consumed.
  • Where the reason is dissatisfaction with market outcomes. Research carries no guarantee of profit, and a loss is not a service failure.
  • Where the subscription is terminated by us for breach of these terms, including redistribution of our research.
  • Taxes already remitted to the authorities.

How to request one

Email info@wmrpl.com from your registered address with the invoice number and the reason. We acknowledge within 1 working day, decide within N working days, and where approved credit the original payment method within N working days. Bank processing time is additional. If you disagree with our decision you may escalate it through our grievance process.
